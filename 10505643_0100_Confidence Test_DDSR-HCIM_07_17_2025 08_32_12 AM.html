Accel X Delta

An out of limits event for this measurement means that the X accelerometer circuit is not functioning. All X vibration measurements should be considered invalid. The test measures the change in output of the MMA1201 accelerometer when it’s internal built in test is executed. The test is initiated by a digital output from MAC7122 PE5 pin to the ST input (pin 4) of the accelerometer. This creates a known force on the internal sense element of the accelerometer. This force should cause a specific change to the voltage output of the device. A failure could be caused by inappropriate values for R32 (4.99K), R36 (10.2K), a blown ADC within the processor, a bad voltage reference (VRH) or a failed accelerometer

Analog 5V

An out of limits event for this measurement means that the 5 volt supply is out of limits. This could effect the magnetometer or gyroscope measurements. This is a secondary measurement and does not indicate a hard failure of the board. An out of limits measurement of this parameter could be caused by inappropriate values for the voltage divider R18 (10.2K) and R19 (10.2K) OR problems with the 5V power supply (T2 or Q9).

Production notes:

The 5V supply is actually set at 4.94 Volts. The correct limits should be 4.84 to 5.04 Volts. The initial voltage limits of 4.9 to 5.1 were incorrect.

CIM I/O Current(mA)

An out of limits event for this measurement means that board is not drawing a reasonable amount of current. This is a secondary measurement and does not indicate a hard failure of the board. This measurement is made by the 1553 CIM I/O card. One must make sure that the DDSR board is the only load on the card at the time of the test. If additional tools are on the subbus, they should be removed and the test repeated. If the build in self test is being performed through the HCIM, this measurement should be ignored.

DDS-R Temp(C)

An out of limits event for this measurement means that board is not reporting a reasonable temperature. The test result should be overridden if the user knows that the board is at the temperature being reported. This can occur if the first functional is conducted at temperatures above 45 C. During oven tests that are controlled by a test system, an error is reported if the measured temperature is outside of 20 degrees from the oven’s target temperature.

Gyro ST1

Gyro ST1:
This is a built in self test of the MEMS gyroscope. It is a built in test of the transducer. It is only run on boards with the gyro installed. A failure of this test is the result of external vibration (oven noise, shop floor noise) or an actual sensor failure. The test is very sensitive.

Production Note:
Errors have been observed when this test is performed in ovens. One must take care that the board is isolated from the oven via foam.

Mag X Delta

An out of limits event for this measurement means that the X magnetometer circuit is not functioning. All RPM measurements should be considered invalid. A failure could be caused by a blown ADC U11, a blown op amp U13, a blown magnetometer U17, blown FETS Q3,Q4, Q5 or a bad SPI bus.

Mag X Linearity

An out of limits event for this measurement means that the X magnetometer circuit is not functioning. All RPM measurements should be considered invalid. The test measures the change in output of the magnetometer when a series of increasing magnetic fields are generated within the device. The fields are created by running a PWM signal into the offset strap of the X magnetometer. This creates a known increase to the magnetic field within the magnetometer. This field should cause a specific change to the voltage output of the device. A failure could be caused by a blown ADC U11, a blown op amp U13, a blown magnetometer U17, blown FETS Q3,Q4, Q5 or a bad SPI bus.

Production notes:

An out of limits event has been seen in manufacturing for the magnetometer linearity test. This event occurs at temperature when the oven is cycling. We believe that the event is caused by magnetic fields generated by the heating coil. It can be identified by a single out of limit value. Another method of identifying the event is by determining how many standard deviations the max/min readings vary from the average. If the max/min readings are greater than 5 standard deviations from the mean, the tool is good.

Subbus (V)

An out of limits event for this measurement means that board is not reporting a reasonable voltage for the subbus. The test result should be overridden if the user knows that the subbus is at the voltage being reported. This can occur if the system is being run from an external power supply rather than a battery. The downhole generator can also cause a high reading. This is because generators output 24 V.
